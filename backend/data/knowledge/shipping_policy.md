# Sunnystep Shipping Policy

## Standard Shipping

- Free standard shipping on all orders over $75.
- Standard shipping: 5–7 business days after processing.
- Orders placed before 2 PM ET on weekdays are processed the same day.
- Orders placed after 2 PM ET or on weekends/holidays are processed the next business day.

## Express Shipping

- Express shipping: 2–3 business days after processing.
- Express shipping fee: $9.99 for orders under $150; free for orders $150+.
- Express cutoff: 1 PM ET on weekdays.

## Overnight Shipping

- Overnight shipping: 1 business day after processing.
- Overnight shipping fee: $19.99 for all orders.
- Overnight cutoff: 11 AM ET on weekdays.

## Shipping Carriers

- Sunnystep uses USPS, UPS, and FedEx depending on destination and service level.
- A tracking number is emailed once the carrier picks up the package.
- International shipping is available to select countries. The total amount during checkout includes all costs associated with your order, including taxes and delivery fees. Taxes are calculated based on the laws applicable to the delivery address.

## Delivery Issues

- If a package is marked "Delivered" but not received, wait 24 hours and check with neighbors or building management.
- If the package is still missing, contact the carrier first using the tracking number.
- If the carrier cannot resolve the issue, contact Sunnystep support with the tracking number and order number.

## Address Corrections

- Address corrections are accepted within 1 hour of order placement.
- After 1 hour, the order enters fulfillment and cannot be rerouted.
- Contact support immediately if you notice an incorrect address.

## PO Boxes and Military Addresses

- PO Boxes are accepted for standard shipping only.
- Express and overnight shipping are not available to PO Boxes.
- APO/FPO/DPO addresses are accepted for standard shipping only.

## Order Cutoff Times

| Service   | Cutoff Time (ET) | Processing Window |
| --------- | ---------------- | ----------------- |
| Standard  | 2:00 PM          | Next business day |
| Express   | 1:00 PM          | Next business day |
| Overnight | 11:00 AM         | Next business day |

## International Shipping

Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship worldwide! Wherever you are, you can enjoy Sunnystep's comfort and support.

How long does shipping take?

- Singapore: 2-4 business days.
- International: 7-14 business days, depending on location.
  Shipping times may vary during peak seasons.

How much does shipping cost?
For Singapore:

- Enjoy free standard shipping on orders over SGD 120.
- A SGD 6 shipping fee applies to orders below SGD 120.
  For international orders:
- Shipping fees vary by country and will be displayed at checkout.

Does my order include duties and taxes?
Yes, the total amount during checkout includes all costs associated with your order, including taxes and delivery fees. Taxes are calculated based on the laws applicable to the delivery address.
