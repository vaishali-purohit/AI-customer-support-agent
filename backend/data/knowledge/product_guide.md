# Sunnystep Product Care and Fit Guide

## Size Guide

- Sunnystep shoes are sized in US men's and women's sizes.
- For half sizes, order the next full size up for a looser fit or down for a tighter fit.
- We recommend measuring both feet and using the larger measurement.

## Fit Recommendations by Model

- **Cloud Walk**: True to size. Narrow foot may consider half size down.
- **Trail Blazer**: Roomy toe box. Order your normal size.
- **City Glide**: Snug fit. Consider half size up if you have a wide foot.
- **Recovery Slide**: One size fits most. If between sizes, size up.

## Material Care

- Mesh uppers: Hand wash with mild soap and cold water. Air dry away from direct heat.
- Leather uppers: Wipe with a damp cloth. Condition with leather conditioner monthly.
- Rubber soles: Clean with a soft brush and soapy water.

## Warranty

- Sunnystep footwear is covered by a 90-day limited warranty against manufacturing defects.
- Warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, misuse, or accidental damage.
- To file a warranty claim, contact support with your order number and photos of the defect.

## Breaking In

- Most Sunnystep models require minimal or no break-in period.
- If you experience discomfort after two weeks of regular wear, contact support for a fit assessment.

## Orthotics and Insoles

- All Sunnystep shoes have removable insoles to accommodate orthotics.
- Removing the insole does not void the warranty.

## Socks and Lacing

- We recommend wearing low-cut or no-show socks with low-top models.
- For hiking models, wear moisture-wicking crew socks.
- Use the alternative lacing patterns included in the product box for customized fit.
