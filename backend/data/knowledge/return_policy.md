# Sunnystep Return Policy

## Standard Returns

- Customers may return unworn items in original packaging within 30 days of delivery.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after inspection.
- Return shipping is free for orders over $75. Orders under $75 incur a $5.99 return shipping fee.

## Exchanges

- Size exchanges are free within 30 days of delivery.
- Exchanges are subject to availability. If the requested size is out of stock, the customer may choose a refund or waitlist.
- Customers may exchange for a different color of the same style at no additional cost if the price is equal.

## Final Sale

- Items marked "Final Sale" are not eligible for return or exchange.
- Final sale items are clearly labeled on the product page and on the packing slip.

## Damaged or Defective Items

- Report damaged or defective items within 7 days of delivery with a photo.
- Sunnystep will replace the item or issue a full refund, including return shipping.
- Do not return damaged items without a prepaid return label issued by support.

## Return Process

1. Initiate a return from the order confirmation email or by contacting support.
2. Print the prepaid return label (if eligible).
3. Package the item securely in its original box.
4. Drop off at any authorized carrier location.
5. Refund is processed after the warehouse receives and inspects the item.

## Refund Timeline

- In-stock item inspection: 1–2 business days after receipt.
- Refund posting: 5–7 business days after inspection.
- Bank or card issuer delays may add 1–3 business days.

## In-Store Returns

- Online purchases may be returned to any Sunnystep retail location.
- In-store returns receive an immediate store credit or refund to the original payment method.
- Online-only promotions may not be valid for in-store returns.
