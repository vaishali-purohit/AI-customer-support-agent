# Sunnystep Account and Order FAQs

## How do I track my order?

- Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number.
- You can also track your order from the "My Orders" section of your Sunnystep account.

## How do I cancel an order?

- Orders can be canceled within 1 hour of placement.
- After 1 hour, the order enters fulfillment and cannot be canceled.
- If you need to cancel after 1 hour, you can refuse the package and return it for a refund.

## Can I change my order?

- Changes to an order are accepted within 1 hour of placement.
- After 1 hour, the order enters fulfillment and cannot be modified.
- Contact support immediately if you need to change an order.

## How do I apply a promo code?

- Enter the promo code at checkout in the "Promo Code" field.
- Only one promo code can be applied per order.
- Promo codes cannot be applied to taxes, shipping fees, or gift cards.

## Why was my payment declined?

- Check that your billing address matches the address on file with your bank.
- Verify that your card has sufficient available credit or funds.
- If the issue persists, contact your bank or try an alternative payment method.

## Do you offer gift cards?

- Sunnystep offers digital gift cards in denominations from $25 to $200.
- Gift cards are delivered by email within 24 hours.
- Gift cards never expire and can be used toward any purchase.

## Is my personal information secure?

- Sunnystep uses industry-standard encryption for all transactions.
- We do not sell or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
- See our Privacy Policy for full details.

## How do I create an account?

- You can create an account during checkout or from the "Sign Up" page.
- An account lets you track orders, save addresses, and access order history.

## Lost Password

- Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.
- You will receive a password reset email within 5 minutes.
- The reset link expires after 1 hour.
